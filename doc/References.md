## Instruments & data

**Kuperman, V., Stadthagen-Gonzalez, H., & Brysbaert, M. (2012).**
Age-of-acquisition ratings for 30,000 English words. *Behavior Research
Methods.*
Role: the measurement instrument for Temporal Coherence. Imported as
`aoa_norms.csv` (31,105 entries). Produced the project's lead empirical result:
492 placement corrections, TC debt reduced from 808 to 429. The result that
leads all academic outreach, on the advice of a forensic linguist who declined
engagement but independently recommended leading with it.

**Roget's Thesaurus (1852 and successors).**
Role: source data. Slips with `roget` / `roget_clean` / `roget_hier` session
ids descend from Roget's classed vocabulary. Roget organized top-down from six
classes; Lexonomy inverts the method — placements are adjudicated one edge at a
time — while inheriting his conviction that vocabulary has an organization at
all.

**Murray, James A. H., and the *Oxford English Dictionary* slip method.**
Role: namesake and working metaphor. The `murray_slips` table is named for the
OED's reading-program slips. The homage is methodological: one item per slip,
evidence attached, adjudicated into a structure by an editor. The OED's slips
established provenance-first lexicography; Lexonomy's two-word self-definition
(vocabulary⟨provenance⟩) is the same commitment applied to placement rather
than attestation.

## Contrast models

**WordNet (G. Miller; C. Fellbaum, ed., 1998).**
Role: the principal contrast case. Synsets are cheap, flat, and permit multiple
hypernyms; the structure consequently over-splits senses (its granularity is a
stock criticism; inter-annotator agreement on its senses runs low). Lexonomy's
placements are expensive, positional, and singular, which biases curation
toward unification — split only when the hierarchy demonstrably cannot connect
the senses (the lexophone doctrine). WordNet's concession of multiple
hypernyms is also the field's standing admission that brains resist unitary
tree modeling; Lexonomy keeps the single-parent discipline anyway, as a forcing
function rather than a mirror-claim (equivocality doctrine).

**OntoNotes sense groupings (Hovy et al.).**
Role: corroborating repair-work. Had to group WordNet senses after the fact to
push annotator agreement to usable levels — post-hoc lumping that Lexonomy's
economics perform natively, at curation time.

**CoreLex (P. Buitelaar, 1998).**
Role: corroborating repair-work. Reconstructed the systematic-polysemy classes
(animal/meat, container/contents) that WordNet's structure scattered. Evidence
that the splits were artifacts of node economics, not of the lexicon.

## Theoretical allies

**Wierzbicka, A., and the Natural Semantic Metalanguage program.**
Role: precedent for a prime set. Decades of empirical testing of semantic
primes across dozens of unrelated languages, finding the set held. The direct
precedent for the 13 primes' claim to universality — and for the expectation
that independently built trees converge near the root. NSM primes are
definitional atoms; Lexonomy's primes are generative floor. Related, not
identical.

**Ruhl, C. (1989). *On Monosemy.***
Role: the strongest theoretical statement of Lexonomy's working posture —
assume one underlying meaning until forced otherwise. Ruhl argued the bias;
Lexonomy's placement economics enforce it. Lump by default, split as a
discovered fact (lexophone).

**Pustejovsky, J. (1995). *The Generative Lexicon.***
Role: ally on unification, rival on mechanism. Dot objects ('book' as
physical•information) build sense-unity into the entry. Lexonomy instead asks
the hierarchy to carry the unity, and registers a lexophone where it cannot.
The GL did reach scale — the EU SIMPLE project built GL-style entries on the
order of ten thousand senses per language — but templatically: entries
self-contained, consistency by shared guideline. Not positional, no
jurisprudence; a placement never competes with another placement (S70
correction of "arguments, not corpora").

**Ramscar, M. — discriminative (error-driven) learning.**
Role: cognitive-mechanism ally for the negation operator. Language learning as
learning what a word does *not* apply to, against the associative
feature-stacking picture. The observable version is child overextension:
"doggie" covers all quadrupeds until 'cat' arrives and carves a piece away —
Bellis's Law running live in acquisition. Bears on the ~S15 extension (all
vocabulary formation as negation), which is severable from the curator's
narrower conjecture ('no' as first organism-to-organism utterance).

**Berlin, B., & Kay, P. (1969). *Basic Color Terms.***
Role: precedent for Temporal Coherence as a cross-linguistic claim. Languages
acquire color vocabulary in a near-universal order — an acquisition-order
constraint on the lexicon holding across languages, which is TC's shape. Also
the template for the replication path: AoA norms exist for Spanish, French,
Dutch, Chinese; the TC methodology is runnable per language, with convergence
near the root as the strong prediction.

## Convergent substrates

*Independent media electing negation as primitive. Instances: vocabulary
(Bellis's Law), braindom (rotated-root essay), logic and silicon (below).
Filed S70.*

**Sheffer, H. M. (1913).** A set of five independent postulates for Boolean
algebras. *Transactions of the American Mathematical Society.*
Role: the functional-completeness theorem. All of Boolean logic is
constructible from the single operator NAND (not-and); one negative primitive
suffices for the whole apparatus of reasoning. Exactly two single operators
are complete — NAND and NOR — both negative, both binary: completeness
requires negation fused with a junction, the anatomy of parent⟨qualifier⟩.
The companion textbook facts, invoked alongside: monotone circuits (AND/OR
only) provably cannot express NOT and therefore cannot express difference —
the proof behind "a yes-primitive lexicon is impossible in principle" — and in
CMOS fabrication the inverting gates are the naturally cheap ones (a plain AND
costs a NAND plus an inverter): hardware builds yes out of no. See the
Negative Primitive entry in ConsciousnessAndVocabulary.md.

## Interface reference model

**NYT Games** (visual design reference — standing doctrine, S70).
Role: not a citation but a model. Solid pills for primary actions, outline for
secondary, content-width buttons, demoted metadata, generous air. Adopted with
the standing exception that pattern breaks where meaning diverges (multi-hue
answer semantics): design is the opposite of rote consistency.
